Hair Oil

Instruction:

  • When: Pre-Wash, 30min-2hrs (1-2x/week), or overnight if tolerated. For Ends: a drop or two on damp hair after washing.
  • How: Warm oil slightly, massage into scalp for 5-10min, cover with shower capif leaving over an hour. You can also lightly add to ends for a refreshing and de-frizzing option.
  • The essential oils (lemongrass, thyme, ylang-ylang,chamomile, cedarwood,lavender, clary sage, patchouli, carrot seed)are potent -- typically 1-3% of the total blend, or a few drops per ounce of carrier.
  • The carrier oils (bhringraj, sweet almond, grapeseed, jojoba, batana, amla, fractionated coconut, rosehip, castor, rosemary, borage seed, argan) make up the bulk of the mix — these are the ones traditionally used for scalp/hair health (bhringraj, amla, castor for growth; jojoba, almond, argan for shine/moisture).
  • Vitamin E oil is usually added in small amounts (a few drops) as a preservative/antioxidant.
